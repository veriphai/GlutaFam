## 1. One-concept statement
**Current flows only across a potential difference between two connected points — no difference, no flow.** (Absolute height above ground means nothing; only the gap between the two points you touch.)

## 2. Guiding-question link + CER contribution
- Episode question (pinned on the board strip all activity): **"The power reaches your town still at lethal transmission voltage — how does it get into your house without killing you?"**
- **Inherits:** 3.1 — supply steps down in stages (the distribution grid); 3.2 — it is CURRENT through a body that harms, and body resistance is roughly fixed. The learner already owns "voltage" and "current" from EP2. **Pins (held constant, visibly):** the upper wire's level never moves all activity (it is drawn static at one height); the bird's/body's resistance is fixed (never said as a number — CURRENT tracks DIFFERENCE in strict lockstep, the felt proof). **Sets up:** 3.4 (insulators prevent the connection) and 3.5 (earthing offers a deliberate easier path to zero).
- **CER contribution: 2 RELEVANT evidence tiles, no distractors from this beat.** `same_level_safe` (no difference → no flow, even high above ground) and `ground_is_zero` (a grounded body spans the full difference) are core to the episode claim — *managed danger: staged step-down + contain + escape route* — because they establish WHEN current crosses a body at all, which is the premise of both containment (3.4) and the escape route (3.5). Recommendation to the EP3 CER owner: source the episode's 2 true-but-irrelevant distractors from 3.1/3.4 detail moments (e.g. "transformers also step down" trivia), not from this beat.

## 3. The felt discovery (discovery → reaction)
The interaction IS the concept: **two contact dots at adjustable electrical heights, with a flow readout between them.** The drawn bird (and later the person) is the skin; the dots are the physics.
- **Discovery 1 (the witnessed absence):** the learner drags the bird onto a high live wire → *reaction:* the feet-dots glow, the readouts wake — `DIFFERENCE 0 · CURRENT 0` — and **nothing happens**. A live wire, a landed bird, no event. The non-event is made felt by the level scale: the wire sits visibly far above GROUND, yet zero crosses the bird.
- **Discovery 2 (the tear-through):** the learner stretches one foot down to a second, lower wire. Mid-air: still nothing (no contact = no second point — the chip literally reads `DIFFERENCE —`). On contact → *reaction, same frame:* a difference spans the bird, a bright current path arcs dot-to-dot through the body, the numbers jump together, the scene flashes. They CAUSED the danger by creating a difference.
- **Discovery 3 (THE discovery — the conceptual centre):** with the bird still bridging both wires, the learner drags the lower wire's level upward. The gap narrows; DIFFERENCE and CURRENT shrink in lockstep; the arc dims continuously. At exact equality the levels snap → *reaction:* **CURRENT hits hard zero the same frame**, the arc vanishes, the bracket on the level scale collapses to a dot — and the bird, still touching BOTH wires, high above ground, is fine. No difference, no flow.
- **Discovery 4 (the payoff case):** the learner raises a grounded person's hand to the wire. Mid-air: nothing. On contact → *reaction:* the FULL difference (wire level minus ground zero) lands across the body; CURRENT slams to its maximum of the whole activity; the path lights hand → body → feet → and onward **along the ground** (the return path made visible). Ground is the reference zero — being connected to it is exactly the danger.

## 4. Felt vs named vs numbered
- **Felt:** electrical level as literal height (wires drawn at their level; ground line at the bottom); the two glowing contact dots; the difference as a glowing **bracket** on the level scale spanning the two dots (collapses to a single dot at zero); the current arc through the body, intensity ∝ flow; the same-frame tear-through and the same-frame zero-snap; the return-path glow running along the ground; the bird-bracket (tiny) vs person-bracket (full scale) contrast.
- **Named:** "LIVE WIRE" and "GROUND" labeled on sight (objects). *Potential difference* is named only AFTER the zero-snap is felt; then the payoff — it is the **same quantity as voltage**; then *ground as the reference zero*. The load-bearing water-between-two-heights analogy appears once, after the naming. No depth-stop line — this beat is fully explainable and explained.
- **Numbered (live, relative, ≤2):** `DIFFERENCE 0–100` and `CURRENT 0–60` (fixed ratio 0.6 so the two chips never display the same value; the lockstep IS the fixed-resistance pin). Mono chips, dark glass, co-varying — the driver/result pattern. When fewer than two contact points exist, `DIFFERENCE —` (nothing to measure between one point). **No level numbers anywhere** (heights are felt); **no ratings table; no number for danger or safety.**

## 10. Build notes

## 11. Acceptance criteria (pedagogy as testable checks)
1. **Discoverable from interaction alone:** with all top-text suppressed (debug flag), a fresh user can read the concept: land bird → nothing; foot to lower wire → current arcs, numbers jump; drag the level → current tracks the gap continuously; equal levels → exact zero while still touching both; grounded hand to wire → maximum current.
2. **Hard gates:** from the frame of any contact break OR the equal-levels snap, CURRENT, arc strands, and path glow are ≤2% within 50 ms — no fade tail; identical under reduced motion.
3. **Connection is required, felt:** while a foot/hand is mid-air, nothing changes — no anticipation glow, no number movement; `DIFFERENCE` reads `—` whenever fewer than two contact points exist.
4. **Held-constant visible:** wire A's height never changes during the whole activity; during level play both feet-dots stay visibly planted and glowing (contact held constant while LEVEL changes); CURRENT and DIFFERENCE move in strict lockstep (fixed ratio — the fixed-resistance pin) and never display the same value.
5. **Exactly 2 live numbers**, relative; no level numbers, no third chip, no ratings table, no number for danger.
6. **The bracket is honest geometry:** its length equals the vertical span between the two active contact dots every frame; it collapses to a dot at zero; the person-case bracket spans the full scale.
7. **Consequence lives in the scene:** the arc through the body is the primary readout (visibly brighter than any chip); the numbers ride alongside and never replace it; the person contact lights the return path along the ground in the same frame.
8. **Naming order:** "potential difference" appears nowhere (text, labels) before T10; the voltage-identity line (T11) only after T10; the water analogy appears exactly once (T12) and is never extended; "zero" for ground appears first at T13.
9. **No pre-spoiling:** no text mentions danger, current through bodies, or the person case before the tear-through is first felt; wire B does not exist on screen until after T2.
10. **Gating:** T4 only on real foot contact (never on proximity); the zero-snap discovery fires only via the learner's drag reaching the snap zone; T15/capture 2 only on real hand contact; the card only after T16.
11. **Register:** the contact moments read grave, not comic — scene dims, heat palette, neutral silhouette, auto-retract; no cartoon electrocution anywhere.
12. **Captures:** exactly two, ids exact, at the §6 moments, idempotent; board strip shows the EP3 question throughout.
13. **Completion contract:** card overlay → Continue → `Navigator.maybePop`; `const BirdOnTheWireScreen()` constructible with no arguments.
14. ≤5 interactive elements; analyzer clean on the activity dir; straight-through run 90–140 s on device; small-phone (360×640 logical) layout sane.

## 12. Anti-patterns (this activity)
- **A meter where the consequence belongs** — the arc through the body IS the readout; chips brighter/larger than the bird would invert the hierarchy.
- **A premium fade on the zero-snap or a contact break** — the same-frame zero IS Discovery 3; any tail muddies "no difference, no flow".
- **Per-wire level numbers** — turns felt heights into arithmetic and busts the 2-number cap; the scale stays numberless.
- **Anticipation sparks while the foot/hand approaches** — would teach "proximity flows", killing the connection half of the concept.
- **Cartoon electrocution / a comedy bird** — register is 12–80, managed danger; the bird never gets X-eyes, the person never convulses.
- **Naming "potential difference" early** (chip label, title, or line before T10) — the name must land on a felt thing.
- **Pre-spoiling the person case** — no "this is what would kill you" before contact is felt.
- **Drag ergonomics as the struggle** — generous hit slop and magnetic snaps; any difficulty must be conceptual, not motor.
- **Letting bird charm outshine the dots** — the dots and arc carry the physics; the bird is skin, drawn simply.
- **Predict-then-reveal quiz chrome** (the old blueprint mechanic) — replaced by direct manipulation; no choice buttons, no ✓/✗.
- **Extending the water analogy** to "electricity travels to your house" or using any analogy beyond T12's single line.

## 13. Open decisions
1. **Person-contact interaction:** drag-the-hand (specced) vs a single tap-to-reach — verify on device that the drag reads grave, not playful; switch to tap-with-slow-rise if it feels flippant.
2. **Chip semantics during the person beat:** specced — the chips re-target to the person pair when the hand drag begins (bird pair stays at 0/0 visually via its collapsed bracket). Alternative: a second chip pair (rejected — busts the 2-number cap); confirm the re-target reads clearly.
3. **Arc streak direction:** downhill bias (specced, matches the water analogy) vs neutral pulsing (AC purism). Propose downhill; no text ever claims a direction.
4. **Current ratio 0.6** — tune on device so the chips feel locked together yet never display equal values.
5. **Wire B clamp** — specced clamped at wire A's level (snap at the top). Alternative: allow overshoot above A with flow reversing (teaches magnitude, risks confusing the "drag up = safe" arc); propose clamp.
6. **Optional soft hum ∝ current (vol ≤ 0.2)** — propose shipping silent; flag for polish.
